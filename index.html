01About

From numbers to governance

My path into cybersecurity started with numbers. A BSc in Mathematics and Statistics taught me to think in risk, probability and structure, and an MSc in Computer and Network Security gave me the technical depth behind it. Serving as an army officer taught me to lead a team, take ownership and stay calm under pressure.

Today I work as an Information Security & Technology Consultant at KPMG Cyprus, with clients in banking, fintech, technology and other sectors. The question that drives my work is simple: how do you know your controls are actually working? Good governance is not about more documents. It is about clear ownership, tested controls and evidence the business can trust.

MSc Thesis · Open University of Cyprus · May 2026

Published

Evaluation of the Quality of Privacy Policy Templates Generated by AI

Can AI tools produce privacy policies that are not only formally aligned with the GDPR, but also clear, complete and understandable?

GDPR Art. 12–14EDPB GuidelinesTransparencyAI Governance
Inside the research

Tools: ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini and specialised privacy policy generators.

Method: each policy was assessed against GDPR Articles 12–14 and the EDPB transparency guidelines, in three scenarios: e-commerce, a health app and an educational platform.

Finding: AI can produce a strong first draft, especially with well-structured prompts, but can create an illusion of legal completeness while relying on vague or generic wording. Human legal review and controller accountability remain essential.

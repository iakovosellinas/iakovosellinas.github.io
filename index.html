About

From numbers to governance

My path into cybersecurity started with numbers. A BSc in Mathematics and Statistics taught me to think in risk, probability and structure, and an MSc in Computer and Network Security gave me the technical depth behind it. Serving as an army officer taught me to lead a team, take ownership and stay calm under pressure.

Today I work as an Information Technology Security Consultant at KPMG Cyprus, across financial services and other regulated sectors. The question that drives my work is simple: how do you know your controls are actually working? Good governance is not about more documents. It is about clear ownership, tested controls and evidence the business can trust.

Long term, I am building towards security leadership, where technical understanding, risk thinking and business communication come together.

Research

Evaluation of the Quality of Privacy Policy Templates Generated by AI

MSc in Computer and Network Security · Open University of Cyprus · May 2026

This research examined whether AI tools can produce privacy policies that are not only formally aligned with the GDPR, but also clear, complete and understandable for the people they are written for. Policies generated by ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini and specialised generators were evaluated against GDPR Articles 12–14 and the EDPB transparency guidelines across three scenarios: e-commerce, a health app and an educational platform.

The findings show that AI can produce a strong first draft, especially with well-structured prompts, but can create an illusion of legal completeness while relying on vague or generic wording. Human legal review and the accountability of the controller remain essential.
